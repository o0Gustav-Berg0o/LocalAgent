# Lesson 11 — Quiz

Write your own answer to each question first, then check it against the answer key below.

## Questions

1. Why does this lesson introduce no new concept?
2. Name three distinct capabilities your finished agent should demonstrate in the full demo run.
3. Why does the retrospective ask you to name your weakest part specifically, rather than just what works?
4. Name two extension points mentioned as deliberately left undone by the course.
5. Why is "teaching one lesson to someone else" suggested as a way to find gaps in your own understanding?

## Answers

1. Because its purpose is to step back and look at everything built across Lessons 1–10 as one whole system, not to add anything new.
2. Any three of: grounded retrieval with citation, an honest "I don't know" on an absent topic, hybrid search finding an exact identifier, a confirmed note creation, a declined note creation that leaves nothing written, a plain conversational reply with no spurious tool call.
3. Reviewing your own build critically, not just functionally, is the actual goal of this lesson — a list of what works without an honest weak point isn't a real retrospective.
4. Any two of: reranking, a real vector database, OpenTelemetry-based tracing, summarization-based history management.
5. Explaining something out loud, in your own words, without the lesson text in front of you, surfaces exactly what you actually understood versus what you only recognized when reading it.
